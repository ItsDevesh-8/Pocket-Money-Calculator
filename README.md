# Pocket-Money-Calculator
This is just a website which calculate your expense 
Pocket

A small personal app for managing pocket money day by day. It’s a single HTML file with no frameworks, no build step and no backend. It installs on a phone’s home screen like an app.

What it does
Daily limit: shows how much is left to spend today, with a bar that fills as you spend. The default limit is ₹70 a day, and you can change it.
Quick add: one-tap buttons for things you buy often, like banana, milk and Coke. You can rename them and change their prices.
Log any expense: enter an amount, note, category and date, including earlier days if you forgot.
Categories: Food, Snacks, Drink, Health, Petrol, Auto and Other.
Carry-over: unspent money rolls into tomorrow, and overspending is deducted. It can be turned off.
Wallet: add money, deduct a monthly subscription, and see how many days your balance will last.
Client income goal: a monthly target with a progress bar for money you earn from clients.
5-day report: every 5 days it shows where the money went:
spending by category, labelled Good, Watch, Extra or Needs
small spends of ₹30 or less and what they cost per month
repeated items, like how often you bought golgappe
your 5 biggest spends
a comparison with the previous 5 days
plain-language notes
a downloadable sheet (CSV) you can open in Google Sheets or Excel
Backup and restore: export your data as a JSON file.
Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file, with the data saved in the browser’s localStorage. It works offline once opened and follows your phone’s light or dark theme.

Install on Android

Open the page in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

Why

Small spends of ₹10 or ₹20 add up without you noticing. This app makes them visible so you can decide where your money goes.
